# Community Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, and the publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Discuss code and ideas without attacking people.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and limit participation when needed. Reports can be sent privately to support@armoreum.io. Share only the information needed to explain the concern.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and project community spaces.
